# Reflection
The dataset tells prices of 4 items in 2 cities on 12 different dates. It allows comparison of prices overtime, examine differences between cities, and identify which items faced greater price increases.It cannot be concluded whether these items are affordable or not as we do not have income or wages metrics. Items cannot be differentiated into basic and non basic because there are no classifications for that. Since the dataset is illustrative teaching data rather than actual PBS observations, its findings should not be presented as actual market trends in Pakistan. The most tempting suggestion was comparing basic and non basic items. However, there are no such categories defined. To conduct this comparison reliably, I would need justified classifications of the items.
