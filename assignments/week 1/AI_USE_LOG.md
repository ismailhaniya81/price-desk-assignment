## Stage 2: AI Critique
Date used: November 4, 2026
Tool: Claude
Purpose: Asked AI to challenge my two research questions and give alternatives.
### Prompt used
I am completing a Prices Desk research assignment. Act as a Socratic research coach. Do not write my assessed answers, choose a research question for me, or solve the assignment.

Here are my two everyday price claims and research questions:

Claim 1: Prices have risen extensively.
Research question 1: How have the prices of items changed over time in the mentioned dates in different locations?
Variables I think are needed: date, items, city, price.

Claim 2: Even basic commodities are unaffordable.
Research question 2: Which household items experienced the largest percentage increase in price during the period covered?
Variables I think are needed: date, items, price.

Please:
1. Challenge the ambiguity in each claim and research question.
2. Identify assumptions I may be making.
3. Explain what additional data or variables might be needed.
4. Suggest five alternative research questions that could be investigated using a price dataset.
5. Do not tell me which question to choose. Instead, ask questions that help me evaluate them myself.

Keep your feedback focused on research design, not writing my assignment.

### Summary of feedback
AI challenged the ambiguity in my claims and research questions, identified assumptions I may be making, suggested additional variables to consider, and provided five alternative research questions. It also asked questions to help me evaluate which question is most suitable using my data.
### Key Takeaways for me
1) Affordibility needs income data, which I do not have.
2) I need to define 'basic items' clearly.
3) Avoid comparing prices from very low base prices.
4) Keep the question answerable with the variables I have.
### AI Suggestions Accepted
1) Comparing price changes across cities is possible because the dataset contains two cities.
2) Comparing price changes over time is possible because 12 dates are available.
3) Comparing different items is possible because all four use the same unit.
### AI Suggestions Rejected
1) Comparing basic and non-basic items cannot be done directly because the dataset does not classify items into these categories.
2) Determining whether commodities are affordable cannot be done because the dataset contains no household income or wage information.
